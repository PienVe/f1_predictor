Method and limitations

The original model positions are preserved. Each simulation adds an error pattern from one earlier race to the model scores, then sorts all drivers into a unique finishing order. Errors are matched by relative predicted rank, not driver identity. Races with different field sizes are included: errors are divided by the source field size minus one, interpolated across relative ranks, then scaled to the target field size minus one. This is an approximation, not an observation of extra drivers. Historical models and interval checks use only earlier data.

The middle 80% range uses the 10th and 90th percentiles. With discrete finishing positions it may contain more than 80% of simulated outcomes. New regulations, weather, crashes and team changes can make earlier patterns unrepresentative. Exact-position percentages have not been separately calibrated.
